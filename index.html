AI Daily Vietnam Publisher

The publishing app behind the TikTok channel @aidailyvietnam — AI news, every day.

What this app is

AI Daily Vietnam Publisher is a content publishing tool that posts AI news videos to the TikTok channel @aidailyvietnam via TikTok's Content Posting API. Use of the app is limited to the channel owner.

How it works

  1. The channel owner connects their TikTok account (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  2. The owner chooses a video and caption to publish.
  3. The app publishes the video to the owner's own channel.

Data and privacy

The app only uses the TikTok account data needed to publish (open ID, username, granted scopes) and the videos and captions the owner submits. It does not collect passwords, private messages, contacts, or location data. Full details are in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using the app are in the Terms of Service.
