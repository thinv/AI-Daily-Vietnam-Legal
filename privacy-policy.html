Privacy Policy — AI Daily Vietnam Publisher

Last updated: October 19, 2026

AI Daily Vietnam Publisher ("the App") is a content publishing tool that posts AI news videos to the TikTok channel @aidailyvietnam via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account data: when you authorize the App, TikTok shares your open ID, username, and the granted scopes (video.upload, video.publish). We use this solely to upload videos you choose to publish.
  • Video content: video files and captions you submit through the App for publishing.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect passwords, private messages, contacts, location data, or any data beyond what is required to publish your videos.

How we use data

Account data and content are used exclusively to authenticate with TikTok and publish videos to your own channel. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Data storage & security

OAuth tokens are stored securely with restricted file permissions and are never committed to public repositories. Access tokens expire after 24 hours and are refreshed automatically.

Your rights

You may revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Upon revocation, stored tokens are deleted.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
